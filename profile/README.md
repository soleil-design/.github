# Soleil

### Design. Build. Maintain. Evolve.

Soleil is a Japan-based creative engineering studio.

We design, develop, and maintain websites, applications,
digital tools, and practical solutions.

Our work connects creative design with reliable engineering,
with a focus on flexibility, maintainability, and long-term value.

---

## Our Projects

### Variable Thema
**A flexible WordPress theme framework.**

Variable Thema separates the structural foundation (Frame)
from visual design (Skin), allowing creators to build freely
on a reliable and maintainable foundation.

- Free and open source
- GPL-licensed
- Designed for WordPress creators and agencies
- Currently under review for the WordPress.org Theme Directory

### WordPress Plugins

We develop WordPress plugins and tools that support
website operations, maintenance, and content management.

Projects include:

- Soleil AI Counter
- Soleil Glossary

---

## Our Philosophy

**Freedom in design. Reliability in structure.**

We believe that a solid foundation should support creativity,
not restrict it.

By separating structure, design, and functionality,
we aim to make digital products easier to create,
maintain, and evolve.

## 日本語

株式会社Soleilは、Webサイト、アプリケーション、
デジタルツールなどの設計・開発・運用を行う制作会社です。

デザインの自由と、長く使える堅牢な構造の両立を目指しています。

Variable Themaは、構造（Frame）と意匠（Skin）を分離した
WordPress向けのオープンソーステーマです。

現在、WordPress.org公式テーマディレクトリで審査中です。

**デザインは自由に。構造は、確かに。**
